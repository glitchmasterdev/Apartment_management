Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Information we collect

We collect your name, email address, phone number, emergency contact, tenancy and lease details, M-Pesa payment records, maintenance requests, IP address and device information needed to secure and operate the portal.

How we use it

We use this information to provide tenancy management, rent tracking, payment-proof review, property notices, maintenance handling, account security and support.

Processors

Supabase provides our database, Resend delivers email, and Vercel hosts the service. They process data only to support these services.

Retention and your rights

Payment records are retained for seven years in line with Kenya Finance Act obligations. On request after tenancy ends, account data is deleted within 30 days unless law requires retention. You may request access, rectification or deletion through the Tenant Portal privacy form.

Kenya compliance

We process personal data in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Contact your property manager about privacy concerns.
